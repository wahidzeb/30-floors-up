About 30 Floors Up

30 Floors Up is a cosy, day-by-day survival story. You are stuck on the 30th floor while goofy green Shamblers shuffle around outside. Each day brings a story card, a few actions and one big question: what will this cost me?

You manage water, food, morale and noise, keep your barricade strong and slowly unlock the Siege Rules: simple, real safety tips collected in your Journal. The story moves from the apartment all the way up to the roof.

3 quick tips

  1. Always fill the bathtub on Day 1. 40 litres of water is a huge head start.
  2. Eat the fridge food first. Bread, milk and eggs spoil; tins keep.
  3. Rest after noisy days. Noise brings Shamblers, and resting quietly lets them drift away.
